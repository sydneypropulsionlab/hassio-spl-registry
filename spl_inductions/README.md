# SPL Inductions

Equipment and space inductions for the Sydney Propulsion Lab, run on Home Assistant.

An assessor starts a session on a tablet and shows a QR code. Inductees scan it, enter their details, and follow the checklist on their own phone while the assessor goes through it with them. Both sign, the inductee confirms by email, and the record is kept in the induction registry.

See the Documentation tab for setup.
