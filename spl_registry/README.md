# SPL Registry

Inductions and checklists for the Sydney Propulsion Lab, run on Home Assistant. Both are written on the SPL wiki (pages tagged `record`) and kept here as records.

- **Inductions:** an assessor starts a session on a tablet and shows a QR code. Inductees scan it, enter their details, and follow the checklist on their own phone while the assessor goes through it with them. Both sign, the inductee confirms by email, and the record is kept in the induction register.
- **Checklists** (preflights, QC checks, inspections): someone fills one in with readings, photos and comments and submits it. The result (e.g. Go / No-go) and every answer are kept; corrections are added as amendments.

See the Documentation tab for setup.
